About this alpha & assumptions

Built for the fictional Waggy Tails Dog Daycare. All profiles, owners, bookings and activities are invented. Sample capacity is backed by individual bookings.

  • 40 places per day; seven operating days are assumed for this demo. “Nearest day” means the next available day, searched over the next 28 days.
  • Both core and kennel cough vaccinations must be valid on the attendance date. An expiry date is valid through that date. Dates are recorded, not medically verified.
  • Some existing sample bookings have expired vaccinations to demonstrate blocked check-in. New bookings cannot bypass the checks.
  • Changes save only in this browser. The owner view is a demonstration, not secure authentication or live cross-device sharing.
  • Sample dates are relative to when you first open or reset the demo. Reset refreshes the examples for today.
